PIECE BONUSES

Every move is the dice sum plus the moving piece's flat bonus, always fully applied:

  • Creature +0 · Dwarf +1 · Man +2 · Elf +3 (Race pieces - forward only)
  • Mind +1 · Will +2 · Body +3 · Soul no bonus, but wins by reaching the Infinite (Person pieces - forward or backward)

SOUL STATUS

A few judgment calls made to get this playable — flag anything that's wrong and it can be adjusted:

  • The 4 central-ring spaces are modeled as a single set passed through once per lap (not twice), sitting between the two loops in the sequence. If Nefesh's real geometry passes through center twice per figure-8 lap, this needs revising.
  • Movement uses only the sum of both dice — d1 and d2 alone, and their difference, aren't usable as a move length. A piece's bonus is a flat add, always fully applied on top of that sum (not a range you choose from).
  • The 4 Race pieces (Creature, Dwarf, Man, Elf) start the game already on the board instead of on the bench — no placement needed for these. Only the 4 Person pieces (Mind, Will, Body, Soul) still need to be placed.
  • Placement can happen on your turn even after the dice are rolled (rules say placement must happen before the roll — simplified here so the turn structure stays simple; you can self-enforce the stricter timing at the table).
  • The Soul's Infinite entry requires landing on an exact target space; overshooting keeps it circling in the endgame zone until an exact roll lands it there.
  • A non-Soul piece that would complete a full circuit is captured automatically instead of completing the move.
